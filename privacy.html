Privacy Policy – Pathshala Control

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Pathshala Control is a private application used only by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload and schedule the owner's videos, read and reply to comments on the owner's videos, and read the owner's channel statistics.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens and fetched data are stored only on the owner's private server. They are never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
